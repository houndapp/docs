Expand description

The shared secret guarding the backend’s /internal routes (RELAY_HMAC_SECRET in its pod env).

Explicit flag/env wins; otherwise --cluster reads it out of the backend Deployment’s pod spec, exactly the way the database URL is resolved. There is deliberately no third source: an internal route is not something to reach with a guessed default.
