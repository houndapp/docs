before:/after:/during: values and the clock they are resolved against.

A DateSpec is a calendar value — a day, a month, a year, or a relative keyword — with no instant attached. It only becomes a millisecond range once paired with a DateContext, because “today” and “the 9th” start at midnight where the reader is, not where the server is. The offset therefore travels with the search request; nothing in this crate reads a clock.

Boundaries are exclusive of the named period, matching how the words read: before:2026-01-15 is everything earlier than that day, after:2026-01-15 everything later than it, and during:2026-01-15 the day itself.
