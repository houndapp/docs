The moq track name a base-layer view is derived from.

Camera source tracks only, matching hound_media::base_view_offered — that function is the receiver’s half of the same decision and this is the relay’s, so the test below pins them equal rather than trusting two lists to agree.

Screen is excluded because halving a share’s frame rate is not a rung anyone wants (its ladder degrades by legibility, and it already spends frames to buy resolution); cam_l0 is excluded because its rungs are already at the frame-rate floor for a recognisable face, and deriving it would double the relay’s per-object work for the rung that benefits least.
