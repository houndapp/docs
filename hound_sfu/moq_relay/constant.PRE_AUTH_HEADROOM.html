const PRE_AUTH_HEADROOM: usize = 4;
Expand description

How many times SessionLimits::max_sessions the pre-auth population may reach before new connections are refused. SessionRegistry deliberately admits only authenticated sessions (so the SIGTERM drain never waits on a stranger), which leaves the accept→auth window uncapped: every accepted connection costs a task plus quinn connection state for up to auth_deadline + AUTH_REJECT_DRAIN. Headroom is generous — a mass reconnect after an SFU restart is legitimate and must not be refused — the point is a ceiling that exists at all, and a counter that makes hitting it visible.
