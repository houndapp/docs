§Demand-driven simulcast planning (sender side)

Aggregates every receiver’s QualityRequest into the sender’s LayerPlan: publish the source while anyone can take it, plus at most ONE shared low layer sized for the most-constrained renderer. plan_layers is the pure fold; SimulcastPlanner tracks the standing per-peer requests and reports plan changes so a steady call costs nothing.

Structs§

LayerPlan
What a sender should publish for a video track given ALL its receivers’ requests (plan_layers). At most two streams: the source and one shared low layer.
SimulcastPlanner
Producer-side aggregator: tracks each receiver’s standing QualityRequest for one published video track and yields the current LayerPlan via plan_layers.
