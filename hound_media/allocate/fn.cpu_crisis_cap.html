pub fn cpu_crisis_cap(source: VideoSource) -> QualityRequest
Expand description

The ceiling a receiver that is short of CPU may spend a freed budget up to, as a cap a caller can fold into a demand with most_constrained.

Once call::policy::DecodeBudget has cut below the platform ceiling, the shell folds this into every gathered tile’s demand so the bitrate freed by hiding tiles cannot buy the survivors a rung whose DECODE is what the receiver just evidenced it cannot afford — see allocate_video’s “a cut must not fund an upgrade” note.

The cap is per-content, and deliberately not “the richest below-source rung”:

  • Camera — the richest below-source rung (crate::LOW_RUNG, 640×360@20). Everything below the source is cheap to decode, so the only thing worth forbidding is the 720p30 source itself.
  • Screen — the cheapest rung (crate::SCREEN_LOW_RUNG, 640×360@15), not the richest. crate::SCREEN_MID_RUNG is 1280×720@30: ~8× the decode work of the floor, and within a rounding error of the 1080p30 source’s. Capping a CPU-crisis share at the ladder top would leave the clamp doing nothing at all for the single most expensive decode leg in the call, which is the one it was written for.

Both are named through low_rungs, so this can never name a rung that ladder does not have.
