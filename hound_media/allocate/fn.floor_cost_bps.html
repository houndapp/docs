What it costs to give every SERVED tile its cheapest rung — the output of allocate_video’s floor phase, priced. The budget controller needs it for two unrelated jobs: it is the target the join-time seed reaches for (BudgetObservation::floor_bps), and it is the line the demote dwell measures “below” against (BudgetConfig::demote_dwell_us).

Same decode_budget contract as demand_ceiling_bps, and here it is the more consequential of the two: the join seed raises a fresh budget outright to this figure, so floors belonging to tiles the decode cut has already hidden would hand the survivors a budget sized for a gallery that is not being rendered.
