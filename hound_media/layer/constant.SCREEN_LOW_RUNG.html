The floor below-source screen rung. Screen share degrades by DegradePriority::Legibility — drop frames before pixels — so a small share tile keeps 360p (a 180p thumbnail of shared text is unreadable, where a 180p thumbnail of a face is fine) and gives up frame rate instead: 15 fps is the same floor the screen ladder’s legibility ordering uses. ≈200 kbps against the 1 Mbps it used to cost, at identical resolution.
