Expand description

How many remote video peers a call must have before a peer that has been observed silent is priced a rung below what its tile alone would buy.

20, which is where Jitsi’s conference-last-n-limits { 20 = 20, 30 = 15, 50 = 10, 90 = 2 } starts tightening: selectivity is a function of room size, because a small call can simply afford everyone and the visible cost of being wrong about who matters (a 2× grid cell filled from a 180p thumbnail is a noticeable 2.25× upscale of a face) is not worth paying there. At 8 peers the size-derived rungs cost ~3.4 Mbps and are affordable; at 30 they are ~12 Mbps and are not. Below this line ranking only ever promotes.
