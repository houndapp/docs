pub fn fit_encode_area(source: (u32, u32), target_pixels: u64) -> (u32, u32)
Expand description

Fit a rung’s PIXEL BUDGET to the source’s own aspect ratio, even-clamped and never upscaled — the area-based sibling of fit_encode_dims.

Use this where the source aspect is unknown in advance and the rung’s cost is what must be held constant: a screen share can be 16:9, a portrait phone mirror, or a near-square window, and a long-edge budget prices those wildly differently (a 640-long-edge square is 640×640 = 1.78× the pixels of 640×360 while being charged the same). Holding area instead keeps the rung’s price honest for every aspect: 16:9 and 9:16 sources land on exactly the same geometry a 640 long edge gave them, and only the near-square case — the one that was mispriced — moves.

fit_encode_dims stays the right tool for a FIXED-aspect source (the camera), where a rung height is the ladder’s own unit and area follows from it.
