pub(crate) fn camera_bitrate_bps(height: u32, fps: u32) -> u64
Expand description

Target bitrate (bits/s) for one CAMERA rung at height×fps, from a bits-per-pixel model — the camera-content twin of screen_bitrate_bps.

Calibrated for MOTION VIDEO, and sub-linear in pixels. A linear-in-pixels model anchored at 720p30 would price a 320×180 thumbnail at ~57 kbps, well under what a legible talking head costs; the pixels^0.70 term instead yields ~130 kbps at 320×180@15 and ~420 kbps at 640×360@20, which is where three independent shipped ladders sit:

  • LiveKit’s presets: h180 = 320×180 @ 160 kbps, h360 = 640×360 @ 500 kbps, with simulcast defaulting to h180 + h360 + primary.
  • Daily’s published defaults: 80 kbps / 10 fps / 320×180, 200 kbps / 15 fps / 640×360, 680 kbps / 30 fps / 1280×720.
  • Jitsi’s bridge: 180p thumbnails, 360p on-stage.

The same numbers show up as published per-tile budgets: Zoom quotes 2.0 Mbps for a 25-tile gallery and 4.0 Mbps for 49 (~80 kbps/tile), and LiveKit reports a 25-person meeting at 4 Mbps (~160 kbps/tile).

Rounded to 10 kbps rather than round_rung_bps’s 100 kbps: the thumbnail rung is only ~130 kbps, where a 100 kbps quantum is a 25% distortion.
