§Congestion control + adaptive quality

Sans-IO pieces for graceful degradation under congestion:

  • RateController — a loss-based AIMD bandwidth estimator (the well-known WebRTC loss controller): increase slowly when loss is low, hold in the middle band, and back off fast when loss is high. Produces a target send bitrate.
  • QualityLadder — maps an available bitrate to a discrete video Rung with hysteresis (drop fast, climb slow) and a manual ceiling, falling back to Quality::AudioOnly when even the lowest rung won’t fit.

Principle: audio is sacred, video is the release valve. This governs video only; audio runs at a fixed low bitrate underneath and is never starved. Both types are pure and clock-driven for deterministic tests.

Constants§

CAM_BPP_K 🔒
Calibration constant for camera_bitrate_bps, solved so the model reproduces the shipped SOURCE rung exactly: 1280×720 @ 30 fps → 1500 kbps (CAM_BITRATE in crates/app/client/src/call/mod.rs). The whole camera ladder is therefore anchored on the one rate the client already publishes at.
CAM_PIXEL_EXP 🔒
How a CAMERA rung’s rate scales with pixel count. Sub-linear for the same reason the fps term is: a small picture needs proportionally more bits per pixel (the per-macroblock and header overheads amortise worse, and downscaling concentrates detail), which is why the industry’s 180p thumbnails are priced at ~3× the bits-per-pixel of their 720p sources rather than 1/16th of the bitrate. Solved from the two anchors below — see camera_bitrate_bps.
FPS_EXP 🔒
How a rung’s rate scales with frame rate. Sub-linear: halving the frame rate does not halve the bits, because the residual between consecutive frames grows as they get further apart. Shared by both content models.
LADDER_FPS 🔒
Candidate ladder frame rates, ascending.
LADDER_HEIGHTS 🔒

RESUME_BACKOFF_MAX_SHIFT 🔒
Cap on the resume-dwell doubling. A link whose true capacity sits below rungs[0] cannot be fixed by a margin alone: every probe genuinely fits the estimate, puts real frames on the wire, and is knocked straight back down — a self-sustaining blink. Each resume that fails to settle doubles the dwell (3 → 6 → 12 → 24 calls), so the sender converges on “stay audio-only, probe rarely” instead of blinking, while a link that recovers for real still gets a probe within the capped window.
RESUME_DWELL_TICKS 🔒
How many consecutive QualityLadder::update calls must clear RESUME_MARGIN before video resumes. The estimate that drives a resume is built with no video traffic on the link (see RateController::on_feedback_idle), so it is the weakest evidence the controller ever acts on; requiring it to hold for a few ticks costs a second-scale delay on a genuine recovery and removes the single-tick flip.
RESUME_MARGIN 🔒
Headroom above rungs[0] required to RESUME video after Quality::AudioOnly. Dropping video is deliberately margin-free (drop fast), but resuming at the bare rung-0 bitrate puts the link exactly at the edge that just failed, so the first real frames re-collapse it — a stable on/off blink. Mirrors the rung-to-rung climb_margin: prove the link can carry the lowest rung plus headroom before putting traffic back on it.
RESUME_SETTLED_TICKS 🔒
Video ticks after which a resume counts as SETTLED — the link really could carry rung 0, so the next collapse starts a fresh episode at the base dwell rather than inheriting the old backoff.
