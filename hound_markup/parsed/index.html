ParsedMessage: the write-side form of a message.

A stored message is its source plus a handful of columns derived from that source. Every one of those columns — the hound: reference index, the link list the unfurler works from, the ContentKind facets a search filters on, and the flattened search_text — is produced here, in one pass, from one parse. Nothing else may compute them, which is what makes “the stored row equals a fresh ParsedMessage” an invariant a reindex can restore rather than a convention that drifts.

§What the source cannot say

Two facets depend on facts outside the body: whether the attachments are pictures or files, and what the message’s links unfurled to. Those arrive as a MessageContext, separately from the parse, because they change on their own clock — an unfurl completes seconds after the message is stored and must not re-parse or re-validate the body to add its facets. That is ParsedMessage::with_context.
