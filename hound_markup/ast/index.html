The parsed message tree: plain owned data safe to cross the FFI boundary.

Document is comrak’s AST translated into hound’s fixed vocabulary (see [crate::parse] for the mapping and its escape hatches). Nothing here borrows from the source string or from comrak’s arena: recursion is only through Vec, so every type is plain owned data — no lifetimes, no references, no maps — and round-trips through flutter_rust_bridge unchanged.
