Mix-tick pacing: the absolute deadline schedule (TickPacer) and the per-platform coarse wait (CoarseWait) that keeps its release precise.

Structs§

CoarseWait 🔒
The coarse half of TickPacer::pace: wait most of the remaining time, which the pacer’s busy tail then finishes precisely. Acquired once per pacer, never per tick — the tick path must not allocate or open handles.
TickPacer 🔒
The bus tick’s clock: an ABSOLUTE deadline schedule with a precise wait.
