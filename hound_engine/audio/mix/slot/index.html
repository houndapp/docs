The per-peer slot — jitter buffer → sample carry-queue — and the summed, soft-limited mix chunk the bus drains from a snapshot of slots each tick.

Structs§

MixFrame 🔒
One decoded audio frame in a slot’s jitter buffer: the sender’s capture-clock timestamp (for playout scheduling) plus its 48 kHz mono PCM. The pump pushes these; the RT thread pops the due ones into the carry-queue.
MixScratch 🔒
Everything one mix tick reuses so the RT bus thread allocates nothing. Owned by the bus loop for the life of the thread and threaded through mix_chunk.
PlayoutState 🔒
The per-peer jitter state the PUMP mutates (push) and the RT thread reads (pop), behind one mutex held only for the bounded buffer op. Owning the [AdaptiveJitter] here keeps push (fold arrival → retarget depth) and pop (the release schedule) operating on one consistent buffer under one lock.
PullOutcome 🔒
What Slot::pull_into reports back to the bus loop for one tick.
Slot 🔒
One producer’s per-peer slot. The PUMP pushes decoded, timestamped PCM into state.buffer (the jitter buffer); the BUS THREAD pops the due frames on its own real-time clock into carry and drains MIX_CHUNK_SAMPLES per tick. The two mutexes are each held only for a bounded op, never across IO; the producer is the async per-peer pump, the consumer is the bus’s own thread (neither is a cpal RT callback).

Functions§

chunk_to_frame 🔒
Wrap a mixed 10 ms chunk in the PcmFrame shape the output sink and Apm::process_render take. The timestamp is unused on the render/playout side (the APM render path ignores it; the cpal ring is time-agnostic), so 0 is fine.
mix_chunk 🔒
Pull one mixed MIX_CHUNK_SAMPLES-sample chunk from the snapshot’s slots at local time now_us: each slot releases its due jitter-buffered frames (on the RT clock) into its carry-queue and accumulates a chunk in i32 (no overflow on N loud peers), then soft-limits the sum and converts to i16. A slot with nothing due contributes silence. Returns the mixed chunk.
