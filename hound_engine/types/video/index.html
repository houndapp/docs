Video frame & packet vocabulary: pixel layouts, codec identity, the raw capture frame (and its zero-copy platform surface), and encoded output.

Structs§

CapturedSurface
An opaque, zero-copy handle to a captured frame still living in its platform capture buffer (an Apple IOSurface-backed CVPixelBuffer).
EncodedChunk
One encoded output unit from a video encoder (an AV1 temporal unit / an H.264 access unit). Carries the keyframe flag and the source timestamp so the packetizer can stamp the wire header straight from it.
RawFrame
One captured raw video frame, stamped on the shared capture clock.
VideoCodecCaps
Which of the three video codecs a device supports, as a small copyable record. Used both as a device’s local encode capabilities and as the running intersection of every receiver’s decode capabilities — the two inputs to the codec ladder (pick_encode_codec).
