Downscale one 8-bit plane by exactly 2 in both axes.

This is the geometry production actually runs — the 1280×720 → 640×360 simulcast LOW rung, and most tile downscales — and every I420 plane inherits it (chroma halves alongside luma). At exactly 2:1 the bilinear convolution scale_i420 otherwise performs collapses to a fixed separable 4-tap [1,3,3,1]/8 kernel: a tent of radius 1, its support scaled by the 2× ratio, lands taps at source offsets ∓1.5 and ∓0.5 with those weights. Hard-coding it drops fast_image_resize’s per-call coefficient derivation (three resizes per frame, one per plane) and its generic convolution inner loop, at bit-for-bit-comparable output — pinned to ≤1 LSB by halve_matches_bilinear_convolution.

It is not a 2×2 box average. That is the 2-tap interpolation bilinear, a different filter; on real frame content the two differ by up to 31 LSB (see box_average_is_not_the_bilinear_kernel, which exists to stop the “obvious simplification”).
