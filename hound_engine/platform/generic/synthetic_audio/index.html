§Synthetic audio source (mic-free test source)

An [AudioInput] that fabricates a continuous SPEECH-LIKE signal at the engine’s fixed 20 ms/48 kHz mono PCM contract, without any microphone — the audio counterpart to [super::synthetic::SyntheticCapturer]. Lets a headless client (a bot with no audio hardware) still publish a mic track.

§Why not a plain sine — the VAD trap

This used to emit a bare 440 Hz tone, and that quietly defanged a CI gate. Opus gates DTX (discontinuous transmission) on SILK’s voice activity detector, not on energy, and a mathematically pure steady sine is the textbook non-speech signal: a single spectral line, no envelope, no noise floor. Measured, the old tone was coded for ~1 s and then suppressed to 1-byte comfort-noise packets for ~77% of frames.

Frame counts kept growing (suppressed packets are still published), so no test turned red — but the receiver’s mix slot then released sub-SILENCE_FLOOR audio, so crate::audio::starve correctly classified every synthetic peer as SILENT and stopped counting dry carries. That is exactly the signal hound_client::call_health gates on, so expect_steady_audio and houndbot’s nightly verdict lost their teeth against real stutter. A test source has to stay audible to the codec, not merely non-zero.

§The signal

Three ingredients, each earning its place against the VAD (see [sample_at]), and all a pure function of the absolute sample index so the waveform is bit-identical run to run:

  • Voiced harmonics — a [F0_HZ] fundamental plus 2nd/3rd harmonics whose weights rotate slowly, giving a moving spectral centroid (a crude formant sweep) instead of one static line.
  • Syllable envelope — [SYLLABLE_HZ] amplitude modulation, the rate real speech energy rises and falls. It dips to [ENV_FLOOR], never to silence: genuine speech gaps would be legitimately DTX-suppressed, which is the behaviour we are trying to keep out of the harness.
  • Noise floor — deterministic LCG dither ([NOISE_GAIN]). Real capture always has one, and broadband energy is the strongest single cue moving SILK off “pure tone”.

§Timing contract

Mirrors [super::synthetic::SyntheticCapturer::next_frame]: [Self::next_frame] paces itself to one [SAMPLES_PER_FRAME]-sample frame by sleeping the remainder of the 20 ms interval since the last emit — so the delivered cadence reflects real time (a real mic’s callback rate) rather than free-running as fast as the caller pulls.

Constants§

ENV_FLOOR 🔒
Floor of the syllable envelope, as a fraction of full amplitude. NOT zero: a true silence gap is legitimately DTX-suppressed, and suppression is the thing this source exists to avoid (see the module doc).
F0_HZ 🔒
Voiced fundamental. A speech-range pitch rather than concert A — with the harmonics below it reads as a steady vowel/buzz, still obviously synthetic to a human listening in on a bot call.
FORMANT_HZ 🔒
Rate at which the harmonic weights rotate, sweeping the spectral centroid. Deliberately incommensurate with SYLLABLE_HZ so the two never lock into a short repeating pattern.
FRAME_INTERVAL 🔒
One 20 ms frame interval — the engine’s fixed audio framing (see FRAME_MS).
NOISE_GAIN 🔒
Broadband noise floor, as a fraction of full scale (~2%). Enough to look like a real capture’s noise floor to SILK; far too low to be heard as hiss over the voiced part.
SYLLABLE_HZ 🔒
Syllable rate of the amplitude envelope. Real speech energy rises and falls at roughly 3–5 Hz; the VAD’s energy-dynamics test keys on exactly this.
VOICED_PEAK 🔒
Peak amplitude of the voiced part, as a fraction of full scale — audible without clipping or reading as a hot/faulty signal.
