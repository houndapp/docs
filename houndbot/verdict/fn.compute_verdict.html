Expand description

Compute the verdict from the run’s observed signals.

Pass iff peers_seen >= expect_peers AND video_frames_rx >= min_video_frames AND no audio starvation was observed. audio_frames_rx is reported but not itself gated (a starvation signal is the actual audio-health gate — a call can be audio-only and still be healthy).
