The realtime plane: wire protocol plus both ends’ reducers, sans-IO.

This is the non-media half of hound’s live traffic — messages, space layout, voice roster, screenshare — carried over whatever transport the client can get (WebTransport where it works, a WebSocket where it does not). Nothing here touches a socket, a clock, or a database:

  • proto is the wire contract, JSON-tagged and versioned.
  • hub is the server reducer: room membership in, addressed messages out.
  • session is the client reducer: it owns the desired-subscription set and reconciles it on every reconnect.

The IO shells (the backend’s api::realtime, the client’s realtime shell) own connections, authentication, and the database lookups; they feed these reducers already-resolved facts and route what comes back.
