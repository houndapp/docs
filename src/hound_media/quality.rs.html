1//! The receiver→sender quality-request vocabulary.
2//!
3//! [`QualityRequest`] is the shared demand type: receivers derive one per track
4//! ([`crate::layer::tile_quality_request`], [`crate::downlink::DownlinkDemand`],
5//! [`crate::allocate_video`]), the sender folds them via
6//! [`crate::simulcast::plan_layers`], and `hound_control::proto` mirrors it on
7//! the wire. Kept free of any machinery so every consumer can name it without
8//! pulling a policy module in.
9
10/// One receiver's standing quality request for a video track, as the sender sees it.
11///
12/// Demand-driven simulcast: the sender publishes only the **source** until a receiver
13/// proves it cannot keep up and asks for less. The request is deliberately
14/// constrained — a receiver asks for AT MOST one tier, and only *below* the source
15/// (it can never ask for more than the publisher captures; a cap at/above source is
16/// just "I can take source").
17#[derive(Debug, Clone, Copy, PartialEq, Eq)]
18pub enum QualityRequest {
19    /// This receiver handles the source/top quality — no lower layer needed for it.
20    Source,
21    /// This receiver can sustain at most this many bits/sec; it wants a lower layer.
22    Capped(u64),
23    /// This receiver is **not rendering** the track at all (its tile unmounted or
24    /// scrolled out of the viewport). It subscribes to nothing for it — zero
25    /// bandwidth, zero decode, zero threads — but it still reports the demand,
26    /// because the producer needs it for two things: the hidden receiver must NOT
27    /// constrain the shared low layer that receivers who *are* rendering depend on,
28    /// and when EVERY requester is hidden the carried value (the cap it would ask
29    /// for if it were visible) sizes the layer rather than letting it fall back to
30    /// source. That second half is what keeps the thumbnail rung published while a
31    /// grid is scrolled away, so a tile coming back into view is a subscribe rather
32    /// than a wait on a producer spin-up.
33    ///
34    /// See [`crate::simulcast::plan_layers`] for how the fold treats it, and
35    /// `call::subscribe::pick_layer` for the receive-side half.
36    Hidden(u64),
37}
