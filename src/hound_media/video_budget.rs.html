1//! # The receive-side total-video-budget controller
2//!
3//! AIMD on the receiver's **total** video budget, driven by a DEFICIENT/STABLE
4//! verdict ([`downlink_deficient`]) over what each subscribed track delivered
5//! versus what [`crate::allocate_video`] granted it. [`VideoBudget`] climbs
6//! additively (one thumbnail rung per probe), backs off multiplicatively on a
7//! sustained deficit, seeds a fresh join to the tiles' floor cost, and holds a
8//! demote dwell so a sawtoothing estimate never blinks the bottom tiles.
9
10use crate::allocate::{VideoAllocation, price, request_bps};
11use crate::layer::{THUMB_RUNG, VideoSource, rung_bps};
12use crate::quality::QualityRequest;
13
14/// Starting total video budget: **4 Mbps**, which is the figure LiveKit publishes for
15/// a 25-person meeting and roughly double Zoom's quoted 2.0 Mbps for a 25-tile
16/// gallery. It is a *probe*, not a measurement — [`VideoBudget`] walks it up and down
17/// from here — but the starting point decides what a 32-peer join costs in its first
18/// second, which is the moment a call is most fragile.
19pub const VIDEO_BUDGET_START_BPS: u64 = 4_000_000;
20
21/// The budget floor: two thumbnail rungs, rounded up. Below this a receiver is not
22/// having a video call, and letting the AIMD walk further down would only feed the
23/// deficit phase tiles to blank.
24pub const MIN_VIDEO_BUDGET_BPS: u64 = 300_000;
25
26/// **8 Mbps**, ≈60 thumbnail rungs — the ceiling on the whole controller, and it wears
27/// two hats deliberately rather than being split into two numbers that would have to
28/// be kept in a fixed relation to each other:
29///
30/// * the **join seed** ([`BudgetObservation::floor_bps`]) may raise a fresh budget to
31///   whatever its tiles' floors cost, up to here. Without that, a 40-peer join spends
32///   its first pass at [`VIDEO_BUDGET_START_BPS`] — under the 5.2 Mbps those forty
33///   thumbnails cost — so the deficit phase hides ten tiles, and because a fresh
34///   `LayerHysteresis` has no history to debounce against, all ten blank immediately
35///   and ramp back one tick at a time. Seeding to the floor makes the receiver's first
36///   answer "everyone small", which is what the floor phase exists to say.
37/// * the **hard cap** on the probe. The budget is spent in PRICED rungs, and priced
38///   spend systematically overstates received bits (a `Capped` tile receives the
39///   producer's cross-receiver-minimum shared layer, which is usually cheaper than the
40///   cap it asked for), so a clean receiver can climb past what its link has ever
41///   evidenced. [`BudgetObservation::ceiling_bps`] does not stop that: it is a DEMAND
42///   ceiling, and 60 tiles asking for the source raise it to 90 Mbps. 8 Mbps is above
43///   any gallery a real receiver renders and below any link this could mislead.
44pub const VIDEO_BUDGET_START_CAP_BPS: u64 = 8_000_000;
45
46/// One subscribed video track's delivery evidence for this pass, read off the
47/// per-pipeline downlink state the reconcile loop already samples — always as a PAIR:
48/// what the track is managing to deliver, and what it was allocated.
49#[derive(Debug, Clone, Copy, PartialEq, Eq)]
50pub struct TrackDelivery {
51    pub source: VideoSource,
52    /// This track's own hysteretic delivery conclusion
53    /// ([`crate::downlink::DownlinkDemand::current`]): `Source` while frames are
54    /// arriving, a `Capped` bitrate once a full evaluation window has seen most of
55    /// them fail to.
56    pub delivered: QualityRequest,
57    /// The rung [`crate::allocate_video`] granted this tile for the pass that produced
58    /// `delivered` — priced at its temporal view, so a base-layer subscription is
59    /// compared against the ~70% of the source it actually asked the wire for and
60    /// not against the full track. Reading it at full price would make every
61    /// healthy `_t0` tile look permanently starved.
62    pub allocated: VideoAllocation,
63}
64
65/// DEFICIENT, in LiveKit's `StreamAllocator` sense: **any** subscribed track is
66/// failing to deliver what this receiver ASKED IT FOR. One starved track is enough —
67/// the budget is shared, so an overshoot shows up wherever the link happens to drop it
68/// first, not democratically across every track.
69///
70/// ## Why the comparison is against the allocation and not against the source
71///
72/// "This track has capped below its source rung" is the wrong predicate, and it fails
73/// in the direction that blanks a call. A track's cap is a per-track,
74/// per-*sender*-link fact: one remote peer on hotel wifi is capped for as long as they
75/// stay on it, and one uplink-constrained sender's frames never arrive for ANY
76/// receiver. Read as evidence about *this* receiver's downlink it is both sticky and
77/// undamped — the predicate stays true forever, the budget sheds 15% every sustain
78/// window, and a 32-tile gallery walks to the 300 kbps floor in about sixteen seconds
79/// with thirty of its tiles hidden, because of one peer nobody was watching closely.
80///
81/// The receiver-side question is narrower and answerable: *is this track delivering
82/// what the budget bought it?* A peer allocated the thumbnail rung and delivering the
83/// thumbnail rung is not evidence of anything, however far below its own source that
84/// is. Only a track delivering strictly LESS than its allocation says the receiver
85/// asked for more than it can take — which is exactly what the budget is a controller
86/// for.
87pub fn downlink_deficient(tracks: &[TrackDelivery]) -> bool {
88    tracks
89        .iter()
90        .any(|t| request_bps(t.source, t.delivered) < price(t.source, t.allocated))
91}

93/// Tunables for [`VideoBudget`].
94#[derive(Debug, Clone, Copy)]
95pub struct BudgetConfig {
96    pub start_bps: u64,
97    pub min_bps: u64,
98    /// Additive increase per probe step — ONE thumbnail rung, i.e. the budget grows
99    /// by exactly the price of one more small tile each time it is found stable.
100    pub increase_bps: u64,
101    /// Minimum spacing between increase steps. The reconcile tick (500 ms), so a
102    /// recovering link regains one tile's worth of budget per pass.
103    pub increase_interval_us: i64,
104    /// Multiplicative decrease on a sustained deficit. **0.85** — the same backoff
105    /// WebRTC's loss controller applies at 30% loss (`1 − 0.5·loss`), and one step
106    /// sheds ~600 kbps at the starting budget: enough to clear a real overshoot in
107    /// two steps, small enough that a marginal one costs a single tile its rung.
108    pub decrease_factor: f64,
109    /// How long the deficit must persist before the first backoff, and the minimum
110    /// spacing between backoffs after it. One full
111    /// [`crate::downlink::DownlinkConfig::eval_interval_us`], so a single bad window
112    /// — a burst, a reorder, one dropped GOP — can never move the budget.
113    pub deficit_sustain_us: i64,
114    /// The hard ceiling on the estimate and the limit on the join seed — see
115    /// [`VIDEO_BUDGET_START_CAP_BPS`].
116    pub cap_bps: u64,
117    /// How long the budget must sit CONTINUOUSLY below the tiles' floor cost before
118    /// [`crate::allocate_video`]'s deficit phase is allowed to start hiding tiles. Until then
119    /// [`VideoBudget::observe`] hands out the floor instead — a bounded, honestly
120    /// priced overshoot.
121    ///
122    /// **1.5 s**, deliberately the same figure as the client's
123    /// `call::policy::LAYER_SWITCH_DEBOUNCE`, and for the same reason: that constant
124    /// is how long a *layer* change must persist before it actuates, and hiding a tile
125    /// is a layer change. Without it, a link whose capacity sits near the floor cost
126    /// makes the AIMD sawtooth straddle that line, and the bottom few tiles blink at
127    /// roughly one cycle every 6–7 s for as long as the call lasts. The hysteresis
128    /// downstream cannot absorb it — the sawtooth is slower than its window.
129    pub demote_dwell_us: i64,
130    /// A gap between observations longer than this is treated as a restart rather than
131    /// as elapsed time. The reconcile loop only allocates while video is live, so a
132    /// paused call (or a backgrounded app) leaves `last_increase_us` arbitrarily stale
133    /// and the first pass after the resume would collect a probe step it did not
134    /// observe a link for. Four reconcile ticks.
135    pub observe_gap_reset_us: i64,
136}
137
138impl Default for BudgetConfig {
139    fn default() -> Self {
140        Self {
141            start_bps: VIDEO_BUDGET_START_BPS,
142            min_bps: MIN_VIDEO_BUDGET_BPS,
143            increase_bps: rung_bps(VideoSource::Camera, THUMB_RUNG),
144            increase_interval_us: 500_000,
145            decrease_factor: 0.85,
146            deficit_sustain_us: 1_000_000,
147            cap_bps: VIDEO_BUDGET_START_CAP_BPS,
148            demote_dwell_us: 1_500_000,
149            observe_gap_reset_us: 2_000_000,
150        }
151    }
152}

154/// One reconcile pass, as [`VideoBudget::observe`] consumes it. Four numbers with two
155/// different vintages, which is worth being explicit about:
156///
157/// * `deficient` and `committed_bps` describe the allocation that was **in force**,
158///   i.e. the one this receiver committed to on the PREVIOUS pass. They cannot be
159///   otherwise: the budget has to be decided before the allocation it produces exists,
160///   so the evidence about an allocation is always one tick behind it. At a 500 ms
161///   tick against a 1 s sustain window that lag costs nothing.
162/// * `ceiling_bps` and `floor_bps` describe the tiles as they are RIGHT NOW, because
163///   they are properties of the roster and the layout rather than of the link.
164#[derive(Debug, Clone, Copy, Default)]
165pub struct BudgetObservation {
166    /// [`downlink_deficient`] over the tracks that had a live pipeline last pass.
167    pub deficient: bool,
168    /// What this receiver is actually committed to receiving right now: the priced sum
169    /// ([`crate::request_bps`]) of the subscriptions the reconcile loop last applied, AFTER
170    /// `LayerHysteresis`.
171    ///
172    /// The controller refuses to cut while this exceeds the budget, and that is the
173    /// point of carrying it. The hysteresis can hold a committed `Source`
174    /// subscription for seconds after the allocator demoted it (1.5 s debounce, 6 s
175    /// dwell); across that window the receiver genuinely IS overspending, so it stays
176    /// deficient — and a controller that reacted would shed 15% per second for a cut
177    /// it had already made and was merely waiting on. Measured at 3.8 → 1.43 Mbps,
178    /// which then blanks cameras for a reason that has nothing to do with them.
179    pub committed_bps: u64,
180    /// [`crate::demand_ceiling_bps`] — the total price of what the tiles are asking for. Gates
181    /// the probe.
182    pub ceiling_bps: u64,
183    /// [`crate::floor_cost_bps`] — what every visible tile's cheapest rung costs together.
184    /// Seeds a fresh budget and anchors the demote dwell.
185    pub floor_bps: u64,
186}

188/// AIMD on the receiver's **total** video budget.
189///
190/// Purpose-built rather than a second [`crate::rate::RateController`]: that one
191/// consumes a loss *fraction* and climbs multiplicatively toward a fixed ceiling,
192/// where this consumes a DEFICIENT/STABLE verdict, climbs additively (one tile at a
193/// time — a total budget shared by N tiles must not jump N tiles' worth in one step),
194/// and re-clamps to a ceiling that moves every pass with what the tiles are asking
195/// for. Reusing it would have meant synthesising a fake loss fraction and calling
196/// `set_max_bps` on every tick, which is a worse description of this than 40 lines.
197///
198/// Sans-IO: `now_us` is the only clock.
199#[derive(Debug)]
200pub struct VideoBudget {
201    cfg: BudgetConfig,
202    budget_bps: u64,
203    /// What the last [`Self::observe`] handed the allocator. Equal to `budget_bps`
204    /// except while the demote dwell is holding the floor above it.
205    allocation_bps: u64,
206    /// `None` until the first observation — a fresh controller must not immediately
207    /// probe above its start value on an arbitrary caller clock.
208    last_increase_us: Option<i64>,
209    /// The previous observation's clock, so a long gap can be told from elapsed time.
210    last_observe_us: Option<i64>,
211    /// When the current uninterrupted run of deficient passes began.
212    deficient_since: Option<i64>,
213    /// When the budget last dropped below the tiles' floor cost, cleared the moment it
214    /// covers the floor again — the demote dwell's clock.
215    below_floor_since: Option<i64>,
216    /// Whether ANY deficiency has been observed on this controller. Latched, and it
217    /// gates the join seed: a receiver with evidence about its link must not have that
218    /// evidence overwritten by a roster that got bigger.
219    saw_deficiency: bool,
220}
221
222impl VideoBudget {
223    pub fn new(cfg: BudgetConfig) -> Self {
224        let budget_bps = cfg
225            .start_bps
226            .clamp(cfg.min_bps, cfg.cap_bps.max(cfg.min_bps));
227        Self {
228            cfg,
229            budget_bps,
230            allocation_bps: budget_bps,
231            last_increase_us: None,
232            last_observe_us: None,
233            deficient_since: None,
234            below_floor_since: None,
235            saw_deficiency: false,
236        }
237    }
238
239    /// Fold one reconcile pass ([`BudgetObservation`]) and return the budget to
240    /// allocate against.
241    pub fn observe(&mut self, obs: BudgetObservation, now_us: i64) -> u64 {
242        // A gap far longer than a tick is not elapsed time this controller watched —
243        // the reconcile loop only allocates while video is live, so a pause of any
244        // length lands here as one enormous step. Restart every clock rather than
245        // paying out a probe step, a backoff, or a dwell expiry earned by an interval
246        // nothing was observed over.
247        let resumed = self
248            .last_observe_us
249            .is_some_and(|t| now_us - t > self.cfg.observe_gap_reset_us);
250        self.last_observe_us = Some(now_us);
251        if resumed {
252            self.last_increase_us = Some(now_us);
253            self.deficient_since = None;
254            self.below_floor_since = None;
255        }
256
257        let last = *self.last_increase_us.get_or_insert(now_us);
258        if obs.deficient {
259            self.saw_deficiency = true;
260            let since = *self.deficient_since.get_or_insert(now_us);
261            // Never cut while the overspend is still PENDING actuation: if the
262            // committed subscriptions cost more than the budget, the receiver is
263            // deficient because a cut the allocator already made has not reached the
264            // wire yet (`LayerHysteresis`). Freeze — the deficit clock keeps running,
265            // so the cut lands the moment the commitment catches up and the link is
266            // still short.
267            if now_us - since >= self.cfg.deficit_sustain_us && obs.committed_bps <= self.budget_bps
268            {
269                self.budget_bps = (self.budget_bps as f64 * self.cfg.decrease_factor) as u64;
270                // Re-arm both clocks: the next backoff is another sustain window
271                // away, and a backoff must not be undone by a probe on the next tick.
272                self.deficient_since = Some(now_us);
273                self.last_increase_us = Some(now_us);
274            }
275        } else {
276            // A single clean pass re-arms the window. That is the anti-oscillation
277            // contract: a link flapping in and out of deficit around a boundary never
278            // accumulates a full sustain window and so never backs off at all.
279            self.deficient_since = None;
280            // Probe only while somebody could use the extra. The ceiling is a gate on
281            // the CLIMB, not a clamp on the estimate — truncating to it would let a
282            // grid scrolled off screen (ceiling ≈ 0 for a pass or two) throw away a
283            // link estimate the receiver would then have to re-earn one rung per tick,
284            // right as the tiles come back.
285            if self.budget_bps < obs.ceiling_bps && now_us - last >= self.cfg.increase_interval_us {
286                self.budget_bps = self.budget_bps.saturating_add(self.cfg.increase_bps);
287                self.last_increase_us = Some(now_us);
288            }
289        }
290
291        // The join seed, until this controller has learned anything about its link:
292        // cover the tiles' floor outright rather than making a 40-peer join hide ten
293        // people and buy them back one per tick. Bounded by the cap — see
294        // [`VIDEO_BUDGET_START_CAP_BPS`] for both of its roles.
295        if !self.saw_deficiency {
296            self.budget_bps = self.budget_bps.max(obs.floor_bps.min(self.cfg.cap_bps));
297        }
298        self.budget_bps = self
299            .budget_bps
300            .clamp(self.cfg.min_bps, self.cfg.cap_bps.max(self.cfg.min_bps));
301
302        // The demote dwell: below the floor, keep handing out the floor until the
303        // shortfall has held for `demote_dwell_us`. The estimate itself is untouched
304        // (so a genuine collapse still walks down), and the overshoot is bounded by one
305        // floor cost and priced honestly — the alternative is the bottom tiles blinking
306        // once a sawtooth.
307        self.allocation_bps = if self.budget_bps < obs.floor_bps {
308            let since = *self.below_floor_since.get_or_insert(now_us);
309            if now_us - since < self.cfg.demote_dwell_us {
310                obs.floor_bps
311            } else {
312                self.budget_bps
313            }
314        } else {
315            self.below_floor_since = None;
316            self.budget_bps
317        };
318        self.allocation_bps
319    }
320
321    /// The link ESTIMATE — what the AIMD believes this receiver can take. Never
322    /// recomputed; a faithful projection of the last [`Self::observe`].
323    pub fn budget_bps(&self) -> u64 {
324        self.budget_bps
325    }
326
327    /// What the last [`Self::observe`] actually handed [`crate::allocate_video`]. Differs from
328    /// [`Self::budget_bps`] only while the demote dwell is holding the floor above a
329    /// collapsed estimate.
330    pub fn allocation_bps(&self) -> u64 {
331        self.allocation_bps
332    }
333}

441    /// A ceiling that collapses does NOT collapse the estimate: a grid scrolled
442    /// entirely off screen asks for nothing for a pass or two, and a receiver that
443    /// threw its link estimate away for that would have to re-earn it one rung per
444    /// tick exactly as the tiles came back into view.
445    #[test]
446    fn a_vanishing_ceiling_does_not_throw_the_estimate_away() {
447        let mut b = VideoBudget::new(BudgetConfig::default());
448        let mut now = 0i64;
449        for _ in 0..20 {
450            now += TICK;
451            b.observe(clean(CEIL), now);
452        }
453        let earned = b.budget_bps();
454        assert!(earned > VIDEO_BUDGET_START_BPS);
455        // Every tile hides: nothing is demanded, so nothing is probed — and nothing
456        // is lost either.
457        for _ in 0..20 {
458            now += TICK;
459            assert_eq!(b.observe(clean(0), now), earned);
460        }
461        // They come back, and the full budget is available on the very first pass.
462        assert_eq!(b.observe(clean(CEIL), now + TICK), earned + 130_000);
463    }

465    /// A sustained deficit backs the budget off multiplicatively, and only once per
466    /// sustain window — a starved receiver walks down, it does not free-fall.
467    #[test]
468    fn a_sustained_deficit_backs_off_once_per_window() {
469        let cfg = BudgetConfig::default();
470        let mut b = VideoBudget::new(cfg);
471        let mut now = 0i64;
472        b.observe(short(CEIL), now);
473        // Inside the sustain window nothing moves, however many passes it takes.
474        while now < cfg.deficit_sustain_us {
475            now += TICK;
476            if now >= cfg.deficit_sustain_us {
477                break;
478            }
479            assert_eq!(b.observe(short(CEIL), now), VIDEO_BUDGET_START_BPS);
480        }
481        now = cfg.deficit_sustain_us;
482        let after = b.observe(short(CEIL), now);
483        assert_eq!(
484            after,
485            (VIDEO_BUDGET_START_BPS as f64 * cfg.decrease_factor) as u64
486        );
487        // The next pass is inside a fresh window: no second cut, and no probe either.
488        assert_eq!(b.observe(short(CEIL), now + TICK), after);
489        // A whole further window later, one more step down.
490        assert_eq!(
491            b.observe(short(CEIL), now + cfg.deficit_sustain_us),
492            (after as f64 * cfg.decrease_factor) as u64
493        );
494    }

496    /// Wobble damping: a link that is deficient every OTHER pass never accumulates a
497    /// full sustain window, so the budget never backs off. Without this a receiver
498    /// sitting exactly on a rung boundary would saw its own budget in half.
499    #[test]
500    fn a_wobbling_link_never_backs_off() {
501        let mut b = VideoBudget::new(BudgetConfig::default());
502        let mut now = 0i64;
503        let mut lowest = u64::MAX;
504        for step in 0..200i64 {
505            now += TICK;
506            let obs = if step % 2 == 0 {
507                short(CEIL)
508            } else {
509                clean(CEIL)
510            };
511            lowest = lowest.min(b.observe(obs, now));
512        }
513        assert_eq!(
514            lowest, VIDEO_BUDGET_START_BPS,
515            "an alternating deficit must never move the budget DOWN"
516        );
517    }

577    /// THE deficiency predicate, and the distinction the whole controller rests on:
578    /// deficient means "short of what I ASKED FOR", never "short of the source".
579    ///
580    /// A remote peer on a bad uplink is capped below its own source for as long as they
581    /// stay on that uplink. Read as evidence about *this* receiver's downlink it is
582    /// permanent, and a permanent deficiency is a permanent 15%-per-second backoff. The
583    /// question that can actually be answered is whether the tile is getting the rung
584    /// the budget bought it.
585    #[test]
586    fn a_track_is_deficient_only_against_its_own_allocation() {
587        use QualityRequest::{Capped, Hidden, Source};
588        assert!(!downlink_deficient(&[]));
589        // Capped to a quarter of its source — and still four times richer than the
590        // thumbnail it was allocated. Nothing about this receiver is short.
591        assert!(!downlink_deficient(&[track(
592            Capped(375_000),
593            Capped(thumb())
594        )]));
595        assert!(!downlink_deficient(&[track(
596            Capped(thumb()),
597            Capped(thumb())
598        )]));
599        assert!(
600            downlink_deficient(&[track(Capped(thumb() - 1), Capped(thumb()))]),
601            "one bit under what it was given IS short"
602        );
603        // A tile allocated the source, and a link that cannot carry it.
604        assert!(!downlink_deficient(&[track(Source, Source)]));
605        assert!(downlink_deficient(&[track(Capped(750_000), Source)]));
606        // A hidden tile costs nothing, so nothing can fall short of it.
607        assert!(!downlink_deficient(&[track(Capped(1), Hidden(thumb()))]));
608        // One starved track among many is enough — the budget is shared, so an
609        // overshoot surfaces wherever the link happens to drop it first.
610        let mut tracks = vec![track(Source, Capped(thumb())); 8];
611        assert!(!downlink_deficient(&tracks));
612        tracks[5] = track(Capped(100_000), Capped(low()));
613        assert!(downlink_deficient(&tracks));
614    }

644    /// The blackout this predicate was rewritten to prevent: a 32-tile gallery with one
645    /// peer whose own uplink has capped them far below their source. Under the old
646    /// "capped ⇒ deficient" rule that single peer held the verdict true forever, the
647    /// budget shed 15% a second, and inside twenty seconds thirty of the thirty-two
648    /// tiles were hidden. Here the budget does not move at all: every tile, that one
649    /// included, is delivering the thumbnail rung it was allocated.

651    fn one_peer_on_a_bad_uplink_cannot_blank_the_gallery() {

657            floor_cost_bps(&tiles, None),
658        );

660        let mut now = 0i64;
661        for step in 0..200i64 {

693    /// Never cut while the previous cut is still in flight. The allocator demotes in
694    /// rung space instantly; `LayerHysteresis` actuates it up to seconds later, and
695    /// across that window the receiver genuinely overspends and so genuinely stays
696    /// deficient. A controller that reacted would compound a cut it had already made
697    /// (measured: 3.8 → 1.43 Mbps, blanking cameras for a reason unrelated to them).
698    #[test]
699    fn a_backoff_waits_for_the_previous_one_to_reach_the_wire() {
700        let cfg = BudgetConfig::default();
701        let mut b = VideoBudget::new(cfg);
702        let mut now = 0i64;
703        // Deficient throughout, and committed to more than the budget the whole time:
704        // the demotion has not actuated. Ten seconds of it — six sustain windows.
705        for _ in 0..20 {
706            assert_eq!(
707                b.observe(
708                    BudgetObservation {
709                        deficient: true,
710                        committed_bps: VIDEO_BUDGET_START_BPS + 1,
711                        ceiling_bps: CEIL,
712                        floor_bps: 0,
713                    },
714                    now
715                ),
716                VIDEO_BUDGET_START_BPS,
717                "the budget moved while its own cut was still pending actuation"
718            );
719            now += TICK;
720        }
721        // The subscription finally lands. The link is still short, and the deficit
722        // clock has been running the whole time — so the cut is due immediately.
723        assert_eq!(
724            b.observe(
725                BudgetObservation {
726                    deficient: true,
727                    committed_bps: VIDEO_BUDGET_START_BPS,
728                    ceiling_bps: CEIL,
729                    floor_bps: 0,
730                },
731                now
732            ),
733            (VIDEO_BUDGET_START_BPS as f64 * cfg.decrease_factor) as u64
734        );
735    }

762    /// …but the seed is bounded, and it fires only until the link says something. A
763    /// 100-tile roster costs 13 Mbps of thumbnails, which is not evidence that anyone's
764    /// link can carry 13 Mbps — the cap is what stops a roster from inventing a budget.
765    /// And once a deficiency has ever been observed, the receiver has real evidence and
766    /// the seed must never overwrite it.
767    #[test]
768    fn the_join_seed_is_capped_and_never_fires_twice() {
769        let tiles: Vec<TileRequest> = (0..100).map(|p| cam(p, QualityRequest::Source)).collect();
770        let floor_bps = floor_cost_bps(&tiles, None);
771        let ceiling_bps = demand_ceiling_bps(&tiles, None);
772        assert!(floor_bps > VIDEO_BUDGET_START_CAP_BPS, "{floor_bps}");
773        let mut b = VideoBudget::new(BudgetConfig::default());
774        b.observe(
775            BudgetObservation {
776                ceiling_bps,
777                floor_bps,
778                ..Default::default()
779            },
780            0,
781        );
782        assert_eq!(b.budget_bps(), VIDEO_BUDGET_START_CAP_BPS);
783
784        // A sustained deficit cuts the budget, and the seed is done for good — the
785        // receiver now has evidence about its own link, and a roster cannot outvote it.
786        let cfg = BudgetConfig::default();
787        let mut now = 0i64;
788        for _ in 0..6 {
789            now += TICK;
790            b.observe(

796                },
797                now,
798            );

807                    floor_bps,

836    /// The demote dwell: a budget that saws across the floor cost faster than the dwell
837    /// never hides anybody. Without it the bottom-priority tiles blink once per
838    /// sawtooth for as long as the link sits near the floor, which is indefinitely —
839    /// and `LayerHysteresis` cannot absorb it, because the sawtooth is slower than its
840    /// window.
841    #[test]
842    fn a_budget_sawing_across_the_floor_never_demotes() {
843        let tiles: Vec<TileRequest> = (0..32)
844            .map(|p| cam(p, QualityRequest::Capped(thumb())))
845            .collect();
846        let floor_bps = floor_cost_bps(&tiles, None);
847        // Tuned to saw: every deficient pass cuts (no sustain) and every clean pass
848        // more than undoes it, so the budget crosses the floor every single tick —
849        // twice per second, well inside the 1.5 s dwell.
850        let mut b = VideoBudget::new(BudgetConfig {
851            start_bps: floor_bps,
852            deficit_sustain_us: 0,
853            increase_interval_us: 0,
854            increase_bps: 700_000,
855            ..BudgetConfig::default()
856        });
857        let mut now = 0i64;
858        let mut crossings = 0;
859        for step in 0..200i64 {
860            now += TICK;
861            let budget = b.observe(
862                BudgetObservation {
863                    deficient: step % 2 == 0,
864                    committed_bps: floor_bps,
865                    ceiling_bps: floor_bps * 2,
866                    floor_bps,
867                },
868                now,
869            );
870            crossings += i32::from(b.budget_bps() < floor_bps);
871            let out = allocate_video(&tiles, budget, None);
872            assert!(
873                out.iter().all(|r| !r.is_hidden()),
874                "step {step}: a tile was hidden at budget {budget} (estimate {})",
875                b.budget_bps()
876            );
877        }
878        assert!(
879            crossings > 50,
880            "the estimate never actually sawed: {crossings}"
881        );
882    }
