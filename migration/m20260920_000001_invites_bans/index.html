The two ways membership of a community is controlled from outside it: an invite (a shareable code that grants entry) and a ban (a standing refusal of one user).

A ban is keyed by (community_id, user_id) and holds no membership of its own: removing someone is still community_member’s job, and this row is only what stops them coming back. Keeping the two apart is what makes a ban survive the kick, the leave, and every later re-join attempt.

An invite carries its whole policy (expires_at, max_uses, revoked_at) rather than a status column, so the derived state — active / expired / exhausted / revoked — is a function of the row and the clock, and cannot drift from a background sweeper that hasn’t run. uses is incremented by a conditional UPDATE, which is what makes the last seat race-safe.

Times are big_integer Unix milliseconds, matching every other timestamp in this schema (upload.created_at, link_embed.expires_at, …): one representation on both backends, and the wire form clients already parse.
