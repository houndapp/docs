How to reach the realtime plane.

A client calls this once at startup: a webtransport_url means “try WebTransport first, fall back to the WebSocket if it fails”; its absence means “go straight to the WebSocket”. cert_hash is present only when the endpoint runs a self-signed certificate (development) and is the SHA-256 digest the client must pin — in production the endpoint carries a real certificate and the field is absent.
