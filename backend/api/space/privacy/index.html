What is_private means on a channel, as a pure policy.

The flag is a label; the rule behind it is a single row — an @everyone deny of channels:view on the space. [Api::filter_accessible_spaces] resolves overwrites and never reads the flag, so the two must be written together or the padlock and the ACL disagree.

Two surfaces edit that one row from opposite directions: PATCH /space/{id} sets the flag and the ACL follows, PUT /space/{id}/overwrites submits the ACL and the flag follows. Both sides of the translation live here so they cannot drift, and both are decided without touching the database.
