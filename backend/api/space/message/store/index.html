backend::api::space::message

Module store 

Source
Expand description

The single writer for a message row and everything derived from it.

A stored message is one source string plus four derived projections: its hound: reference rows, its [ContentKind] rows, its flattened search_text column, and its attachment links. Api::store_message is the only place any of them is written, and it takes a whole [ParsedMessage] rather than the pieces — so a body and the columns that describe it are physically incapable of disagreeing.

§The invariant

Every derived column equals what a fresh [ParsedMessage] would produce from the row’s current contents and its current attachments and embeds. Nothing else may write them, and Api::rederive_message restores the invariant for one message from those three inputs alone — which is what makes the whole projection rebuildable (POST /internal/messages/reindex) and what lets an unfurl landing minutes later add its image/embed facets without touching the body.

§Why the writes are replace-all

Delete-then-insert, not a diff. The sets are tiny (a handful of rows), and a full replace is what makes an edit that removes a reference, a facet, or an attachment unable to leave its row behind.
