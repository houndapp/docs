Module organiser 

Source
Expand description

Instance-operator installation and removal of community organisers.

The ordinary way into @organisers is the role-membership routes in super::role, and they are organiser-only by the role hierarchy: handing out the role that holds every community permission cannot be reachable from ManageRoles. That rule leaves one case unserved — a community whose organisers are all gone, or one being handed to someone new — because there is nobody inside it left to grant anything.

These two routes are that case, and they answer to the instance instead: ManageCommunities is the capability that creates and deletes communities, so installing an organiser in one is the same kind of act. The community’s own organiser-only rule is untouched on the role routes.

Granting is join-then-install: an operator names a user who may never have been a member, and an organiser who is not a member is a role row nothing can act through. A ban still refuses — the community’s standing refusal outranks an instance permission, exactly as it does on PUT /community/join.
