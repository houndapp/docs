Module ban 

Source
Expand description

Community bans: a standing refusal of one user, independent of whether they are currently a member.

A ban is two things done together — the removal (the same teardown a kick does) and the row that stops them coming back. Keeping the row out of community_member is what makes the refusal outlast the removal: leaving, being kicked again, or redeeming a fresh invite all consult the ban table, not the membership one.

The ordering inside post_ban is the same one the kick path documents: the transaction commits, MemberRemoved goes out while the banned user’s connection is still in the room to hear it, and only then are their rooms revoked.
