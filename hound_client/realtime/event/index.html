Pending 🔒
Callers waiting for a space’s subscribe confirmation. Owned by supervise, not by one session: a subscribe taken while the plane is down (or one whose connection dies before the answer) is answered by the NEXT session’s confirmation, since the reducer re-issues the whole desired set on every Welcome.
