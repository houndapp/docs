The transport cluster: leg selection (WebTransport-first, WebSocket fallback), the two concrete pipes, the WT skip gate, and the URL/credential plumbing.

The production Dial: the REST client is the config source, the credential source, and the origin both legs are derived from.
WsPipe 🔒
The universally-reachable leg. Mirrors the backend’s WsPipe: text frames are the protocol, and ping/pong/binary are skipped rather than treated as frames, so a proxy’s keepalive can never desync the stream. They are still COUNTED — a pong is the only evidence a silent peer is alive.
WtGate 🔒

Caps on what a peer can make this client buffer, matched to the frame cap the WebTransport leg’s length-delimited codec enforces and the backend applies to both legs. max_message_size bounds a reassembled fragmented message, max_frame_size a single frame — either alone leaves the other unbounded.
