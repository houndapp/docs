Module test_tone

Expand description

The speaker check: a short sine played through a [PlayoutBus] — the same mixed-playout path (device open, hot-swap fallback, output cushion) a call’s remote audio takes — so “I heard it” vouches for the output a call will use.

Out of call only: the bus built here has no AEC render reference, so a tone during a call would reach the far end through the microphone. The refusal lives at the command seam (crate::HoundClient::play_test_tone).

Constants§

DEVICE_OPEN_LEAD 🔒
Head start the bus gets to open the device and prefill before the first frame is pushed. Frames that come due while the device is still opening would be released in one burst into the slot’s ~100 ms carry and lose the tone’s head.
DRAIN_TAIL 🔒
Held after the last frame is due so the device ring drains before the bus (and with it the output stream) is dropped.
DURATION_MS 🔒
Tone length.
FADE_MS 🔒
Linear fade at each end; without it the step onset/offset clicks.
FRAME_SAMPLES 🔒
Samples per pushed frame: 20 ms, the call’s audio frame size.
FRAME_US 🔒
FRAME_SAMPLES in µs on the bus clock.
FREQ_HZ 🔒
Tone pitch: concert A.
LEVEL_DBFS 🔒
Peak level. Loud enough to hear over a fan, quiet enough not to startle on headphones at a high OS volume.
TONE_FLOOR_US 🔒
Jitter-buffer floor for the tone’s slot. The whole tone is pushed at once, so its newest frame lands floor + 280 ms ahead of now — under the playout buffer’s 400 ms catch-up ceiling, past which it re-anchors and skips the head.

play 🔒
Play the tone on speaker_id (None = host default) and return once it has drained. A device that fails to open is the bus’s own reopen ladder’s business — the bus reports nothing back, so neither can this.
tone_samples 🔒
The tone as 48 kHz mono PCM: FREQ_HZ at LEVEL_DBFS peak for DURATION_MS, ramped in and out over FADE_MS.
