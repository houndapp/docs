Re-announce this client’s decode caps whenever they change mid-call, so the roster — and every producer negotiating against it — learns about a codec that appeared (or went away) without the user rejoining.

Event-driven, not polled: crate::openh264::availability_changes wakes on the only transitions this process can actually observe — Cisco’s blob installing, and the settings kill switch either way. A capability that appears with no notification behind it (a VAAPI device hotplugged mid-call) is still missed; the wire and reducer carry it the moment a trigger for it exists, and re-probing blind on a timer would open a VAAPI display every tick for a transition that essentially never happens.

Re-announcing is a diff against what went out last, not a re-send: a wake that leaves the caps unchanged (a failed download, a toggle onto the state we were already in) puts nothing on the wire, and the server suppresses a no-op update again on its side. A reconnect needs none of this — a fresh Hello re-probes.
