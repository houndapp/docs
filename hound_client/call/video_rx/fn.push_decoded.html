Hand one DECODED frame to the render sink, surface-first (zero-copy GPU handle) with materialized I420 as the universal fallback. Consumes the frame, so the no-surface case MOVES the decoder’s conversion buffer instead of copying ~1.4 MB per frame per peer (push_preview is the borrow-based capture-side twin).
