const FIRST_KEYFRAME_DEADLINE: Duration;
Expand description

How long a fresh video subscription may sit with nothing DECODED before the pipeline ends itself so the reconcile re-subscribes (restart-ladder paced). Past the nag window (5 × 2 s): a producer that ignored every re-request is wedged or its broadcast is a zombie from a same-path replacement, and only a fresh subscribe — a new announce cursor + control-plane Subscribe — heals those; waiting on the 60 s safety-net IDR is a frozen tile.

The deadline is armed by a DECODED frame, not by a keyframe reaching the gate: a keyframe in a codec this platform has no decoder for arrives perfectly and decodes never, so satisfying the latch pre-decode disarms every recovery this pipeline has and leaves the tile black for the rest of the call. Restarting against a sender still stuck on that codec is churn, but the reconcile’s [RestartPacer] backs it off exponentially, and bounded churn is the price of ever recovering at all.
