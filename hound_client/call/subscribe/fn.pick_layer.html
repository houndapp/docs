Which track of one (peer, video source) group to subscribe to for the committed demand req. Pure, so the fallbacks are unit-tested.

The roster is the ONLY liveness signal available here, and the producer keeps it truthful: it withdraws the _l0 [TrackDecl] whenever it deactivates the low encoder (see [super::simulcast::LowDecl]). So:

  • a capped demand takes the low track only when its decl is present — otherwise the source, which is always producing. Subscribing to a decl-less or torn-down layer yields nothing and costs that tile the full VIDEO_STALL → FIRST_KEYFRAME_DEADLINE → restart-ladder walk (minutes of black video);
  • a low track WITHOUT its source is never picked at all. The producer’s source encode is never dropped (the low layer is purely additive), so a lone _l0 is by construction stale — its owner stopped publishing that video and only the source’s Unpublish has reached us so far.

When the low decl (re)appears the reconcile switches to it through the normal LayerHysteresis path — source→low is a downgrade, so it is debounced.
