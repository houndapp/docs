Turn any outstanding keyframe request into a throttle-approved forced IDR, and arm the encode loop’s force_keyframe seam when one is due. Pure (time passed in) — the throttle itself lives in [hound_media::KeyframeThrottle].

Called from BOTH the immediate path (a RequestKeyframe just arrived — see AdaptShared::pli_notify) and the 1 s adaptation tick, so the two can never disagree about what a request means. Idempotent: with nothing pending and nothing due it is two relaxed loads.

The immediate path is what makes subscribe→IDR fast. Waiting for the tick cost up to a full ADAPT_TICK_INTERVAL of pure latency, which a screenshare — [SCREEN_KEYFRAME_CADENCE] emits IDRs on demand only, so no periodic keyframe bails it out — paid in full on every layer switch and every loss resync.
