Expand description

Fold one restarting exit into the dry-restart streak and decide whether the share may be restarted at all. Returns true to restart, false when the share has been GIVEN UP on (in which case this has already torn it down, dropped the desire, logged the terminal error and published it to the screen-error surface).

This is the fix for the silent-death loop: the first-frame watchdog restarts a capture that never delivered, the re-open succeeds, the source still delivers nothing, the watchdog fires again — forever, at one cycle per SCREEN_FIRST_FRAME_DEADLINE, with a black self-preview and no log line anywhere. delivered_any is the discriminator (only the capture loop can know it), and policy::screen_restarts_exhausted is the bound.
