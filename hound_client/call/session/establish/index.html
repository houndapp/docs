Functions§

establish_live 🔒
Build one full control+MoQ LiveSession. Each establish re-mints the relay token by calling voice/join, so a token-expiry SFU drop heals via reconnect — that re-mint is what keeps a call alive past the relay token’s TTL. The capture loops subscribe to params.devices.* (so device selection survives a reconnect) and read params.mute/params.camera_enabled. Seeds the identity map from the fresh voice/join each time.
spawn_mute_sync 🔒
Keep the ROSTER’s mute flags in step with our local intent for this session’s lifetime. Mute is per-session control-plane state — a fresh establish (and every track republish) starts un-muted — while MuteFlags are call-lifetime, so without this a call that heals while muted goes live to everyone, and every receiver reads the silence of a track it believes unmuted as a wedged subscription (the audio stall watchdog re-subscribes it for nothing).
