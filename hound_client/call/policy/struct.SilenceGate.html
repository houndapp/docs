Expand description

Publisher-side silence suppression: stop putting silence on the wire, keep the subscription warm. Pure (bytes + now in, publish/skip out) so the whole policy is unit-tested without an encoder or a socket.

§Why suppress at the SOURCE rather than unsubscribe at the receiver

The alternative — a receiver that unsubscribes all but the top-K loudest peers — was evaluated and rejected, for the same reason the wire-priority band refuses a dominant-speaker promotion (see subscribe::audio_strictly_outranks_video_on_the_wire): it puts a re-subscribe in the path of the first word. A client-driven resubscribe cannot beat ~0.9–1.1 s best case (a 300 ms report grid, the 500 ms reconcile tick, a subscribe RTT, and the 80 ms playout target), so the first word is clipped every time; a peer who has never won a dominance election has no rank at all and stays unsubscribed through multi-second holes; and audio resubscribe churn on the shared QUIC connection is the documented voice-killing incident class. Suppressing at the source has none of those properties: every subscription stays open, so the first voiced frame rides a connection that is already there — zero attack latency.

§What it actually saves, and why it is worth doing at all

Opus DTX already collapses the PAYLOAD of a silent frame to 1–2 bytes, so the cost of a silent track is ~95% FRAMING, not audio: a 16-byte object header plus moq varints ≈ 19 B every 20 ms ≈ 8 kbps, and a fresh QUIC uni-stream per 4-object group = 12.5 streams/s per track. Across 31 silent remotes that is ~250 kbps and ~390 streams/s of pure silence arriving at each receiver, and the SFU’s O(N²) stream term is entirely indifferent to whether those objects contain speech. Suppression collapses the wire bytes, the stream churn AND the receiver’s decode of silence at once, which no receiver-side policy can do.

§The rules

  • Bytes-driven, never a VAD of our own: the encoder already ran one, and its verdict is legible in the payload size (DTX_MAX_BYTES).
  • Suppression engages only after SUPPRESS_AFTER CONSECUTIVE DTX-sized frames.
  • While suppressed, one real encoder frame goes out per KEEPALIVE_INTERVAL — a genuine DTX packet, never a synthetic one, so the receiver decodes real comfort noise and the stall watchdog sees real arrivals. A coded frame that lands while suppressed IS that interval’s keepalive (it was published, so the wire and the watchdog both saw it); no redundant DTX frame follows it.
  • Every coded frame is published, always, at any point in any run. No coded frame is ever withheld — that is the invariant the whole design rests on, and the reason resume costs nothing.
  • Clearing the suppressed STATE is the one thing that waits: it needs RESUME_AFTER consecutive coded frames, because on a real mic a single isolated coded frame is the comfort-noise refresh, not somebody talking. This delays no audio — only the resumption of publishing the silence around it.
  • Capture timestamps are untouched throughout. The receiver’s gap classification is timestamp-driven (classify_audio_gap) and the jitter estimator is differential over (arrival Δ) − (ts Δ), so real timestamps make a suppression gap cost exactly nothing on both; invented contiguous ones would break both.

§It can never engage on a headless client, by construction

The synthetic capture source every bot and e2e participant publishes is deliberately VAD-ACTIVE — dtx_never_suppresses_the_synthetic_capture_source (hound_engine::codec::opus) pins ≥98% of its frames as coded — so this gate is permanently open for it and the suite’s audio-liveness floors are unaffected. That is a load-bearing property, not an accident: do not “fix” the synthetic source into something quieter. The e2e scenario that exercises suppression injects real silence through SyntheticSilence instead.

Mute is NOT this gate’s business: it gates before encode (audio_tx), so what arrives here is only ever unmuted-but-quiet.

Fields§

§dtx_run: u32

Consecutive DTX-sized frames observed (reset by any coded frame). Only counts on the way IN — once suppressed, the flag below is the state.

§coded_run: u32

Consecutive coded frames observed (reset by any DTX-sized frame). Clears suppression at RESUME_AFTER; see that constant for why one is not enough.

§suppressed: bool

Whether silence is being withheld right now. Explicit rather than derived from dtx_run, because the entry and exit conditions are deliberately asymmetric.

§last_published: Option<Instant>

When we last published — the keepalive clock while suppressed.
