Floor on the live decode budget — Jitsi’s MIN_LAST_N. Below three remote videos a gallery has stopped being a video call, and the audio this protects is long since safe.

The engine clamps its own static ceiling to the same figure (platform’s MIN_DECODE_SESSIONS, which is why the two are duplicated rather than shared: they are the same policy applied on either side of the capability seam, and the engine crate cannot depend on this one). DecodeBudget::new re-clamps regardless, so a platform can never hand this controller a ceiling under its floor whatever the engine reports.
