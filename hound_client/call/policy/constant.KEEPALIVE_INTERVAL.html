How often a suppressed publisher still emits one frame. 500 ms, bounded from both directions:

  • Upper — the receiver’s subscribe::AUDIO_STALL reaps a subscription that delivers nothing for 8 s. A suppressed sender must stay visibly alive, and it must do so with margin over a lost keepalive or two, so the interval has to be a small fraction of that window (16× here).
  • Lower — every keepalive is an object in the moq count-rolled audio group (AUDIO_GROUP_OBJECTS = 4), so at 2/s a group rolls every ~2 s. That keeps the ordered substream ticking over — groups still open and close, late-join still has boundaries to attach at — while costing ~1/25th of the streams a speaking sender opens. Going much slower would leave a group open for tens of seconds; much faster would give back the saving.
