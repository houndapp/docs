Module audio 

Source
Expand description

Audio policy, both directions: capture-side mic route warmup + dead-silence detection, per-stream playback gain application, publisher-side silence suppression (SilenceGate), and the receiver’s gap classification / PLC math / level reporting / cadence diagnostics.

Structs§

CadenceDiag 🔒
Per-stream receive-cadence diagnostic (a retained debugging tool). Accumulates over a window and, on completion, reports the signals that explain an active-stream gap the smoothed jitter number hides — see the call site. Pure bookkeeping; no IO.
CadenceWindow 🔒
One completed CadenceDiag window. A struct rather than a tuple because the fields are read individually by a human reading a log line, and plc vs fec next to each other is the whole point of the pair.
MicRouteWarmup 🔒
One-shot mic route warmup: a non-48 kHz capture device (a Bluetooth headset in HFP mode) may open onto an SCO route that is not carrying audio yet — the stream runs, delivering silence, and no error ever fires. Reopening once, hound_engine::api::ROUTE_WARMUP_DELAY after open, lands on the settled route; 48 kHz devices never arm. The playout side has the same policy inside the mix bus; this is the capture-side half.
MicSilenceWatch 🔒
Detects a dead-silent capture stream (see MIC_SILENCE_WARN_FRAMES). Self::observe returns true exactly once per silent streak, at the threshold; any non-zero sample resets the streak (and re-arms the warning).
SilenceGate 🔒
Publisher-side silence suppression: stop putting silence on the wire, keep the subscription warm. Pure (bytes + now in, publish/skip out) so the whole policy is unit-tested without an encoder or a socket.

Constants§

CADENCE_WINDOW_FRAMES 🔒
How many decoded frames per cadence-diagnostic window (~5 s at 50 fps).
DTX_MAX_BYTES 🔒
The DTX/coded size threshold. Owned by the codec that produces the sizes (hound_engine::api::DTX_MAX_BYTES) and measured against the live encoder there, so the publisher, the receiver and the codec cannot drift apart. Largest encoded payload (bytes) this encoder produces that is DTX output rather than coded signal — the one number that separates “the encoder gave up on this frame” from “the encoder is spending bits”, without anyone having to look at the audio.
KEEPALIVE_INTERVAL 🔒
How often a suppressed publisher still emits one frame. 500 ms, bounded from both directions:
MAX_PLC_FRAMES 🔒
Max consecutive frames concealed with Opus PLC on a timestamp gap. A gap of up to this many frames (~100 ms at 20 ms/frame) is plausibly recoverable brief loss worth concealing; beyond that the gap is the sender being silent (DTX emits no frames) or a capture discontinuity — fabricating extrapolated audio there is wrong (it mislabels silence as loss and splices garbage into an active stream). Silence is played across the larger gap instead.
MIC_SILENCE_WARN_FRAMES 🔒
Consecutive exactly-zero mic frames before warning once that the input is delivering pure silence — a dead capture route (BT/SCO, revoked permission, a silent virtual device) produces bit-exact zeros, which a live room’s noise floor never does. ~3 s at 20 ms frames.
RESUME_AFTER 🔒
Consecutive coded frames required to CLEAR suppression. 3 frames = 60 ms.
SUPPRESS_AFTER 🔒
Consecutive DTX-sized frames before publication stops. 10 frames = 200 ms.

Functions§

apply_gain 🔒
Scale decoded PCM by gain, saturating at the i16 rails (a >1 boost must clip gracefully, never wrap). Unity is the caller’s fast path — call only when gain != 1.0. Pure; unit-tested.
classify_audio_gap 🔒
Classify a timestamp gap. Pure, and the ONE place the PLC-vs-silence line is drawn.
frames_missing 🔒
Frames genuinely missing in a gap_us jump between consecutive in-order frame timestamps (frame size frame_us). Tolerant of sub-frame capture-timing jitter: SCO/resampled sources never space frames at exactly 20 ms, so a gap under ~1.5 frames is the next frame arriving a little late, NOT a loss. Counting it would fabricate one spurious PLC frame per real frame (concealment dwarfing real audio). Pure + unit-tested. The caller separately caps the result at MAX_PLC_FRAMES.
reported_audio_level 🔒
The speaking level to report for a decoded frame, given the size of the packet it came from. A DTX-sized packet (DTX_MAX_BYTES) carries comfort noise, not speech, so it reports 0 rather than metering the noise: without this a suppressed peer’s UI ring sits at whatever the comfort noise happens to measure (the meter is logarithmic, so a −40 dBFS floor already reads ~0.3 of full scale) for as long as they stay quiet — a lit ring on a peer who is saying nothing. More necessary since the capture chain’s loudness contract landed, not less: AGC2’s make-up gain rides the sender’s noise floor up with the speech, so the level a suppressed peer would otherwise report is HIGHER than it used to be. Pure; unit-tested.
