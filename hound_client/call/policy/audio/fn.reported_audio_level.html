The speaking level to report for a decoded frame, given the size of the packet it came from. A DTX-sized packet (DTX_MAX_BYTES) carries comfort noise, not speech, so it reports 0 rather than metering the noise: without this a suppressed peer’s UI ring sits at whatever the comfort noise happens to measure (the meter is logarithmic, so a −40 dBFS floor already reads ~0.3 of full scale) for as long as they stay quiet — a lit ring on a peer who is saying nothing. More necessary since the capture chain’s loudness contract landed, not less: AGC2’s make-up gain rides the sender’s noise floor up with the speech, so the level a suppressed peer would otherwise report is HIGHER than it used to be. Pure; unit-tested.
