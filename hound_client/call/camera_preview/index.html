Module camera_preview 

Source
Expand description

The out-of-call camera preview — what onboarding’s Devices step shows.

One blocking capture thread opening the camera at the call’s capture format (CAM_DIMS@CAM_FPS) and pushing every frame to the "self" stream key through the same push_preview tee the call’s camera loop uses, so the Flutter side binds the preview texture exactly as the in-call self tile does. Nothing is encoded or published.

The call owns the camera. A preview is refused while a call is live and yields (is stopped) when one starts — both at the command seam (crate::HoundClient::camera_preview_start / crate::HoundClient::call_join). This module assumes the device is its own.
