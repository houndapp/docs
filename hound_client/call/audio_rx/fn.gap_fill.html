Plan the fill for an AudioGap::Conceal gap of missing frames after prev_ts; fec is whether the carrier packet is big enough to carry in-band FEC (a DTX-sized one is not — libopus would just conceal internally).

Oldest-first with the FEC slot LAST, and that order is load-bearing: the Opus decoder is stateful, so the recovery must be decoded before the carrier packet it was extracted from. Pure + unit-tested; the pipeline only decodes and pushes what this yields.
