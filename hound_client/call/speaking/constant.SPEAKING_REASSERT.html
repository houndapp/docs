const SPEAKING_REASSERT: Duration;

How long a non-silent report may go unrepeated before the receiver’s ranker would read the peer as gone quiet. The emitter only sends on a change, so a peer talking steadily at one band would otherwise fall off the grid; re-asserting well inside [hound_media::speaking::IDLE_US] keeps a steady speaker steady without paying the full 3 Hz for an unchanging level.

Expressed in TICKS, because the naive IDLE_US / 2 does not survive the grid: a re-assert can only land on a [REPORT_INTERVAL_US] tick, so the real period is this value rounded UP to whole ticks, and the ticker runs MissedTickBehavior::Delay — a late tick pushes the whole grid out rather than being chased. IDLE_US / 2 = 1.0 s therefore lands at 1.2 s and leaves only 0.8 s of slack under the receiver’s 2 s decay, which one scheduler hiccup on a loaded encode thread eats whole (a steady talker then decays mid-sentence and loses the floor it is currently holding). Two ticks = 600 ms leaves 1.4 s, for one extra ~40-byte frame per talking peer per 1.2 s.
