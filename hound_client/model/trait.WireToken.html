Expand description

A closed enum whose variants ARE wire tokens — one spelling, written once.

The token table is the single source of truth: as_wire/from_wire and the serde impls are all generated from it by [wire_tokens!], so a spelling cannot drift between the hand-written mapping and a rename_all attribute (they did, and only one of the two was on the path the backend reads).
