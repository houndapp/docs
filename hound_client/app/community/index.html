Community + channel management commands: the settings surfaces’ write path.

Two kinds of command live here, and the difference is what the caller may assume when one returns:

  • Tree-folding commands (everything that changes a community or a space) fold their own response into the community-tree snapshot before returning, with send_modify. The realtime echo will say the same thing a moment later and the reducers are idempotent, so this is not a source of truth — it is what makes the editor’s own client update at the speed of the request instead of the speed of the socket. Nothing here is optimistic: the fold happens after the server agreed.
  • Fetch commands (members, roles, bans, invites, channel overwrites, instance) return a value and touch no snapshot. Those datasets are paginated or settings-only and rarely open, so they are pulled on demand and refreshed off CommunitySignal rather than reduced. Invites and overwrites have no signal at all — nothing on the wire announces them — so whoever wrote one re-reads it.

Joining is the one case that is both: join_invite and create_community fold the community they return AND fetch its channels, because the sidebar row would otherwise expand to nothing until the next full load.
