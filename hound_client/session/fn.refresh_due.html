fn refresh_due(claims: &TokenClaims, now_unix: i64) -> bool
Expand description

Whether a token with these claims should be refreshed at now_unix — pure, so the whole policy is unit-testable without a clock.

The rule is the half-life: refresh once half the token’s own lifetime has passed, which needs no knowledge of the backend’s 45-minute constant and scales if it changes. Degradations, in order:

  • no usable iat (missing, or not before exp — a clock-skewed or malformed token) ⇒ fall back to a fixed REFRESH_FALLBACK_LEAD window before exp;
  • no readable exp ⇒ never due. We cannot reason about the token at all, and the on-401 retry is the net that catches it.
