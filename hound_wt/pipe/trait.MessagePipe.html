Expand description

A bidirectional, message-oriented, UTF-8 text pipe.

§Contract

  • send delivers one whole frame, or fails terminally for this pipe.
  • recv yields None once the peer closed cleanly or the pipe is finished; Some(Err(_)) is a transport failure and is likewise terminal.
  • recv MUST be cancel-safe: dropping the future (e.g. losing a tokio::select! race) must not consume or truncate a frame. Both implementations satisfy this by keeping decode state in the pipe rather than in the future.
  • close is best-effort and idempotent.
