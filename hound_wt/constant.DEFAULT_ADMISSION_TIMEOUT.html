Expand description

How long a peer may take to get from “the endpoint saw its first packet” to admitted or turned away — the whole handshake + authenticate + accept/reject sequence.

Every step is under the caller’s control except the peer’s own progress, so this is the bound that stops an unauthenticated peer from holding a server task (and, before per-peer admission, the accept loop itself). Matches the SFU’s session-auth deadline.
